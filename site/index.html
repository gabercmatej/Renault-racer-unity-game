RENAULT RACER

A playable Unity Web racing game and campaign concept: an unofficial idea for a lap-time giveaway around the electric Renault 5 Turbo 3E. Race the circuit, set your best lap, beat it.

Open in full window ←→ / AD steer · ↓ / S / Space brake · Shift handbrake · R restart · accelerates automatically

Highlights

  • Custom arcade handling on a fixed timestep, not Unity physics.
  • Pseudo-3D road with hills on a 15-turn circuit.
  • Animated car that follows your steering, with electric motor audio.
  • Boost pickups along the track.
  • Touch-ready: on-screen controls, safe-area layout, rotate-your-phone gate.
  • Cumulative checkpoint timing against your previous best lap. Personal bests stay in your browser; there is no leaderboard.
The in-game interface is in Slovenian. Unofficial concept: not affiliated with or endorsed by Renault. Renault and Renault 5 Turbo are trademarks of Renault. Source code: GitHub.
